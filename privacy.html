Privacy Policy – Ilizin LLC Merch Pins

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Ilizin LLC Merch Pins is a private tool used only by Ilizin LLC to publish Pins of its own original t-shirt designs to its own Pinterest business account. Each Pin links to the matching product page on Amazon.

What data the app uses

  • It connects only to the Ilizin LLC Pinterest account, with that account owner's permission.
  • It reads that account's own boards (to choose where a Pin goes) and creates Pins on them.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of other Pinterest users.

Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer and used solely to publish the owner's Pins.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, Ilizin LLC earns from qualifying purchases made through links in its Pins.

Contact

Questions: message Ilizin LLC on Pinterest at pinterest.com/ilizinllc.
